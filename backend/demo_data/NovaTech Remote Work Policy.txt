NOVATECH REMOTE WORK POLICY

Eligibility

Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with manager approval. Employees in their first 3 months of employment should discuss remote arrangements with their manager before starting.

Core Hours

Remote employees must be reachable during core hours, 10:00 to 16:00 local time, and must attend scheduled team meetings by video.Equipment and Expenses

NovaTech provides a laptop and a one-time home office stipend of $500. Internet costs are not reimbursed. Employees must use the company VPN when accessing internal systems.

Working From Another Country

Working remotely from outside the country of employment is limited to 20 days per calendar year and requires written approval from the People team for tax and legal reasons.
