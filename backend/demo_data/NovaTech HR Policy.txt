NOVATECH HR POLICY

Purpose and Scope

This policy sets out the human resources rules that apply to every NovaTech employee. It is maintained by the People team and reviewed once a year. Where this policy is more specific than other guidance, managers should apply this policy and report any inconsistencies to HR.Parental Leave

Eligible employees receive 16 weeks of paid parental leave following the birth or adoption of a child. Employees must have completed 6 months of service to be eligible. Parental leave must be taken within the first year after the child arrives.

Performance Reviews

Performance reviews are held twice a year, in June and December. Managers must share written feedback with each employee within 10 days of the review meeting.Conduct and Escalation

Employees who observe a breach of the code of conduct should report it to their manager or to the People team. Reports can be made anonymously through the ethics line. NovaTech does not tolerate retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith.
