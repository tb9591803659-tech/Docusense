NOVATECH LEAVE POLICY

Sick Leave

Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for absences longer than 3 consecutive days. Unused sick leave does not carry over.

Bereavement Leave

Employees may take up to 5 days of paid leave following the death of an immediate family member.Parental Leave

Parental leave is covered in the HR Policy and is not restated in this document.

Jury Duty and Public Service

Employees called for jury duty receive their regular pay for the duration of service. Employees should notify their manager as soon as they receive a summons.
