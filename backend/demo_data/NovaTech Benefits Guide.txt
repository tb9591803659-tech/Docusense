NOVATECH BENEFITS GUIDE

Overview

This guide summarises the benefits available to NovaTech employees. Some benefits begin on your first day, and others become available as you complete service milestones.

Healthcare

NovaTech pays 80 percent of the monthly premium for medical, dental and vision plans. Dependants can be added during enrolment or after a qualifying life event.Benefits After One Year

After 12 months of continuous service, employees become eligible for the retirement plan employer match, an annual learning budget of $1,500, and a sabbatical application window. These benefits are not available during the first year of employment.

Retirement

NovaTech offers a 401(k) plan. After one year of service the company matches contributions up to 4 percent of base salary. Employees can change their contribution rate at any time through the benefits portal.
