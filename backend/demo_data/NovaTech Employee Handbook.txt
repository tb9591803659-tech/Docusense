NOVATECH EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK

Welcome to NovaTech

NovaTech builds logistics software for mid-sized shipping companies. This handbook describes how we work together, what you can expect from us, and what we expect from you. It applies to all full-time employees.

Working Hours

NovaTech's standard working week is 40 hours. Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 16:00 local time, during which employees should be reachable by their team.Office Attendance

Employees are expected to be in the office at least 2 days per week. Team leads may set the specific anchor days for their team, and all-hands meetings are held in person on the first Tuesday of each month.

Vacation

Full-time employees accrue 15 days of paid vacation per year. Unused vacation of up to 5 days may be carried over into the following calendar year. Vacation requests should be submitted to your manager at least two weeks in advance.

Health Coverage

Medical, dental and vision coverage begins on the first day of employment.Parental Leave

Eligible employees receive 12 weeks of paid parental leave following the birth or adoption of a child. Employees must have completed 6 months of service to be eligible. Parental leave must be taken within the first year after the child arrives.

Questions

Questions about this handbook should be sent to the People team at people@novatech.example.
